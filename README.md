## Hi, I'm Arman.

I'm an M.Sc. student at **Sharif University of Technology**, working on [blind speech separation](https://arman-majidi.github.io/#research) with Dr. Massoud Babaie-Zadeh.

I study how to recover individual voices when several people speak at once. More broadly, I'm interested in statistical signal processing and in what learning methods can tell us about a signal.

Before Sharif, I studied electrical engineering at the **University of Tehran**. My undergraduate thesis used accelerometer data and a CNN to recognize body postures, under Dr. Saeed Akhavan.

I'm applying for PhD positions in speech, audio and statistical signal processing.

## Research

### Blind speech separation

*M.Sc. thesis, in progress · Sharif University of Technology*

A microphone records everything at once. My research asks how we can recover the individual speakers from overlapping recordings, without knowing the mixing process in advance.
