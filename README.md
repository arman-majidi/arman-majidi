## Hi, I'm Arman.

I'm an M.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering at **Sharif University of Technology**, working on [blind speech separation](https://arman-majidi.github.io/#research) with Dr. Massoud Babaie-Zadeh.

I study how to recover individual voices when several people speak at once. More broadly, I'm interested in statistical signal processing and in what learning methods can reveal about a signal. Before Sharif, I completed my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at the **University of Tehran**, where my undergraduate thesis used three-axis accelerometer data and a CNN to recognize body postures under the supervision of Dr. Saeed Akhavan.

## Research

### Blind speech separation

*M.Sc. thesis, in progress · Sharif University of Technology · Supervised by Dr. Massoud Babaie-Zadeh*

A microphone records everything at once. My research asks how we can recover individual speakers from overlapping recordings without knowing the mixing process in advance.

### Recognizing body posture from sensor data

*B.Sc. thesis, 2025 · University of Tehran · Supervised by Dr. Saeed Akhavan*

I built a 1D-CNN pipeline to classify body postures from three-axis accelerometer recordings — from raw sensor streams and sliding windows to posture prediction.
